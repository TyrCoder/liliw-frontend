03 Content lifecycle

Content Publishing Workflow

Applies uniformly to all nine content types. Authoring and publishing are separate roles by design — an Officer reviews against a snapshot of the last approved version, and editing live content sends it back to Draft.

04 Visitor engagement

QR Check-In & Points Flow

A check-in is credited only after the visitor's location is confirmed against the attraction's coordinates. That single credited visit is what unlocks a review, ticks an itinerary item, and counts toward achievements.

%%{init: {'theme':'base','themeVariables':{'fontFamily':'Plus Jakarta Sans, sans-serif','primaryColor':'#FFFFFF','primaryTextColor':'#1A2340','primaryBorderColor':'#0B3D91','lineColor':'#1A2340','tertiaryColor':'#FBF6EA','edgeLabelBackground':'#FBF6EA','fontSize':'14px'},'flowchart':{'curve':'basis','nodeSpacing':34,'rankSpacing':44}} }%%
flowchart TD

    B --> C[Opens Scan and allows\ncamera access]
    C --> D[Scans the QR code]
    D --> E{Signed in?}
    E -- No --> F[Prompted to sign in\nor create an account]
    F --> D
    E -- Yes --> G[System reads the visitor's\ncurrent location]
    G --> H{Within the required\ndistance of the attraction?}
    H -- No --> I["Check-in rejected:\n'must be at the location'"]
    H -- Yes --> J{Already checked in\nhere recently?}

    L --> Q{Place is on a\nsaved itinerary?}

    Q -- No --> P
    L --> S[Review form becomes\navailable for this place]

05 Business onboarding

LBO Application Flow

Applying requires no account — it is a public form. A signed-in tourist can instead apply from their profile. Either way, approval upgrades the account to LBO and future corrections go through a reviewed change request, never a direct edit.

    D --> E[Fill in business name, owner,\ncontact, address, type,\npermit number - optional]
    E --> F[Submit application]
    F --> G[Status: Pending]
    A2([Already a signed-in tourist]) --> A3[Applies from\nProfile - Apply as LBO]

    G --> H([CHATO Officer reviews\nthe application])

06 AI planning

AI Itinerary Planning Flow

The planner is grounded — it selects only from real, published places, never invented ones. Road distance is calculated by an external routing service when reachable, and falls back to a labelled straight-line estimate when it is not.

08 Business onboarding

LBO Change-Request Flow

An owner never edits their own public listing. Every correction — a phone number, an hour, a photo — is a request CHATO reviews first, keeping the office as the single source of truth for what is published.
